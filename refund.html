7-day refund

If Pro doesn't work for you, email swiftbill26@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase with your Gumroad receipt email, and we will refund the full $9. No long explanations needed — a short note helps us improve.

How refunds work

  • Refunds are issued through Gumroad to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few business days to show it.

  • After 7 days we generally can't offer refunds, except where the law requires it.
