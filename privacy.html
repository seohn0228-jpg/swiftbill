Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

SwiftBill is a small invoice and quote maker run by an independent developer based in South Korea. It is built so that your business data never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you type — your business details, client details, line items, logo, signature and saved invoices — is stored only in your own browser (local storage). We do not run a server that receives or stores it, and we cannot see it. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, it is gone, and we cannot recover it.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Gumroad. Gumroad collects your payment details and email address under its own privacy policy. We receive the order information Gumroad shares with sellers (such as your email address and license key) so that we can support your purchase.

License check

When you enter a license key, SwiftBill sends that key directly from your browser to Gumroad to confirm it is valid, and checks it again about once a week. No invoice data is sent.

Visitor statistics

We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (GoatCounter) to count page views and button clicks such as "Export PDF". It does not use cookies and does not collect what you type.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and keep it only as long as needed to help you.

Children

SwiftBill is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact
